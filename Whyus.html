Our Care Philosophy

Why Choose Vimak Dental Centre?

Vimak Dental Centre provides professional dental care with a focus on patient comfort, oral health and informed treatment decisions. We believe a visit to the dentist should be a calm, respectful, and educational experience where every patient understands their oral health status and available care options.

From routine dental check-ups and oral hygiene scaling to root canal therapy, crowns, bridges, orthodontics, and children's dental care, our clinics in Nairobi (ICEA Building) and Thika (Maisha Heights) are designed to make quality dental care accessible to individuals and families.

  • Professional Dental Care: Thorough clinical assessments and individualized dental treatment planning.
  • Patient-Focused Experience: Clear communication, respectful chairside care, and attention to patient comfort.
  • Dental Education & Prevention: Practical oral hygiene guidance in-clinic and via our educational TikTok channel (@_vimak_dental_centre).
  • Modern Clinical Environment: Well-maintained dental equipment, treatment rooms, and strict hygiene standards.
  • Family Dental Care: Welcoming dental care for children, teenagers, adults, and seniors.
  • Accessible Locations & Extended Hours: Clinics in Nairobi CBD and Thika, open Monday–Saturday (8:00 AM – 8:00 PM) and Sunday (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM).

What Sets Us Apart

Our Commitment to Every Patient

Six core principles guide how we care for patients at our Nairobi and Thika dental clinics.

1. Professional Care

Every treatment plan is grounded in an in-person dental examination and diagnostic evaluation tailored to your specific oral health needs.

2. Patient-Focused Experience

We listen carefully to your concerns, answer your questions without rushing, and prioritize a calm, reassuring atmosphere during every visit.

3. Dental Education

Informed patients make healthier choices. We educate our community on preventive oral hygiene, tooth preservation, and children's dental care.

4. Modern Clinical Environment

Our treatment rooms and dental instruments are maintained with strict sterilization and clinical hygiene protocols.

5. Family Dental Care

Whether it is a child's first dental check-up or restorative care for an adult, we provide compassionate dental services for the whole family.

6. Accessible Locations

Visit us at ICEA Building (JKUAT Towers), 2nd Floor in Nairobi or Maisha Heights Building, Ground Floor, Kenyatta Avenue in Thika.
